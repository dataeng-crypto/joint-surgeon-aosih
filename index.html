In conventional posterior hip surgery, major gluteal muscles and tendons must be cut to reach the hip socket, requiring weeks of healing and strict dislocation precautions. In DAA, Dr. Naveen Sharma accesses the hip through a natural intermuscular plane without cutting any muscle. Patients walk within 4 hours post-op, experience significantly less pain, and face virtually zero dislocation restrictions.
With our subvastus muscle-sparing technique, the quadriceps extensor mechanism remains intact. Most patients stand and walk with walker assistance within 12 to 24 hours of surgery. Stair climbing is initiated under physiotherapist supervision by Day 3 to 5. Most patients discard walker support completely within 2 weeks.
