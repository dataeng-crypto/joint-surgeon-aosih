Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information on reaching AOSIH Jaipur, OPD consultation hours, and transit connectivity.

From Jaipur Railway Junction (4.5 km), take an auto or taxi via Elevated Road (10 mins). From Jaipur Airport (11 km), travel via Tonk Road and New Sanganer Road (20 mins). Alternatively, alight directly at Metro Pillar 95 on the Jaipur Metro.
OPD consultations run Monday to Saturday from 11:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Emergency orthopedic and trauma admissions operate 24x7. Prior appointment booking is recommended for reduced waiting times.
